Our Focus

Four domains.
One research posture.

FloLabs applies AI and robotics research across four areas. Each sits at a different stage of maturity — some are active programmes, others are early-stage exploration — and each is treated on its own terms below.

01Education 02Healthcare 03Travel 04Space
01

Hands-on, project-based learning model

01 — Focus Area

Education

AI-powered educational tools and platforms intended to revolutionise learning.

FloLabs' education work is an experiential platform for AI, robotics and entrepreneurship, aimed at students, career changers and lifelong learners. Participants work on real-world projects in teams, gaining hands-on exposure to ideation, research, development and product evolution — building skills, confidence, problem-solving ability and collaboration experience.

Audience
Students, career changers, lifelong learners
Learning model
Hands-on, project-based, collaborative
Support
Masterclasses — video & podcast content from experts, plus recorded material
Access
Flexible, remote-based learning with global collaboration

02 — Focus Area

Healthcare

Early-stage research and development in AI-powered systems, wearable technology and intelligent automation.

FloLabs' healthcare direction explores predictive analytics, natural-language processing, computer vision and wearable integration — investigating how technology can make healthcare more data-driven, adaptive and responsive while complementing human expertise. Potential applications include personalised care, early detection, process optimisation and remote monitoring.

Early-Stage R&D

This work is explicitly framed as research, not a clinically deployed product.

03 — Focus Area

Travel

Intelligent automation and personalisation intended to improve travel experiences.

FloLabs' travel direction focuses on conversational AI travel assistants that understand preferences, adaptive itineraries that respond to weather and disruptions, and real-time support for delays, cancellations and rerouting. Research also explores sustainability — eco-friendly recommendations, route optimisation and reducing unnecessary emissions.

Conversational AI
Understands preferences, supports planning and decisions
Adaptive itineraries
Respond to weather, disruptions, new recommendations
Real-time support
Delays, cancellations, rerouting, changing needs

04 — Focus Area

Space

AI-driven technologies and innovation connected to space exploration and travel — framing space as both a frontier for exploration and a driver of technology development with implications beyond the space sector.

Education & entrepreneurship

Space-related learning pathways and entrepreneurial opportunity.

Commercial space travel

Future concepts
