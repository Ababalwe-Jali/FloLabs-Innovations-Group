Connected Intelligence

The pattern underneath
CAIPO & MoodChanger.

CAIPO responds to the need for personalised, sustainable ways to optimise health and productivity for individuals and organisations, connecting health data, wearable devices and tailored guidance. MoodChanger for People expands this to smart glasses, rings, watches and insoles, using activity, heart rate, stress, posture and hydration data to generate personalised insights. MoodChanger for Pets applies the same pattern to pet wellness.

AI & Robotics

Seven research areas define
the robotics direction.

FloLabs presents itself as focused on intelligent systems that integrate into human life and industry — intelligent automation, adaptability and human-centric design, with practical applications across healthcare, manufacturing, mobility and service environments.

01

Autonomous systems

Machines capable of navigating environments, making decisions and performing tasks independently.

02

Collaborative robotics

Robots designed to work alongside people in manufacturing, healthcare and services.

03

Precision robotics

High-dexterity systems for delicate or accuracy-sensitive tasks.

04

Human-robot interaction

Improving how robots understand and respond to human behaviour.

05

Machine learning

AI systems that learn from experience, adapt and improve over time.

06

Sensor integration

Cameras, LiDAR and tactile sensing for awareness and decision-making.

07

Responsible robotics

Safety, transparency and ethical design are stated priorities across the research direction.

Research Spotlight

Animal Vocalization

Research using acoustic analysis, behavioural studies and deep learning to investigate animal vocalisations — including potential applications in pet healthcare, behaviour understanding, training assistance and veterinary support. It reinforces a broader FloLabs theme: using AI to interpret complex data and turn it into useful, human-centred experiences, beyond conventional enterprise software.
