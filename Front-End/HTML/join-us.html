Join Us

There's more
than one Way in.

FloLabs is built by people who learn, research, build and collaborate together. Whichever path fits you, it starts with a conversation.

01

Learn

Join the experiential education programme in AI, robotics and entrepreneurship.

Built for students, career changers and lifelong learners — the programme is hands-on and project-based. Teams work through ideation, research, development and product evolution together, supported by masterclass video and podcast content from experts, with recorded material for anyone who can't attend live. Learning is remote-friendly, built for global collaboration.

Ask about the education programme
02

Collaborate on Research

Work alongside FloLabs on AI and robotics research.

FloLabs' research spans autonomous systems, collaborative robotics, human-robot interaction, connected intelligence and applied domains like healthcare and travel. If you're a researcher, engineer or technologist interested in contributing to any of these areas, we'd like to hear what you're working on.

See the research areas
03

Careers

FloLabs grows its team around active research and initiatives.

There isn't always a formal listing for every opening — the fastest way to be considered is to reach out directly, tell us what you do, and point to the focus area or initiative that fits you best.

Get in touch about opportunities
04

Partner

Explore a joint research effort, pilot, or collaboration.

Organisations working in education, healthcare, travel, robotics or space-adjacent fields are welcome to start a conversation about where FloLabs' research direction might intersect with yours.

Propose a partnership
