Who We Are

A collective built on
research, not routine.

FloLabs Innovations Group explores emerging technology the way a research lab does: investigate, experiment, build, and apply. Rather than positioning as a single-service company, FloLabs operates as a multi-domain innovation ecosystem — connecting artificial intelligence, robotics and experiential learning to real problems in healthcare, travel, education and beyond.
